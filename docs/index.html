Type anywhere

Say "Sayso", then talk. Your words appear in whatever app you're in: email, chat, docs, code editors, AI assistants. Free, and it runs on your own PC.

Set up once, in a minute

1Download and install

Run SaysoSetup.exe. No admin rights, no account.

2Check your mic

Pick your microphone and watch the level bar move. Sayso shows you how to allow the mic if Windows blocks it.

3Choose your apps

Sayso lists every app on your PC. Switch off the ones where it should never type.

After that there's nothing to set up again. Sayso starts with Windows and waits by the clock. Click the microphone icon any time to change things.

Private by design

Your voice stays on your PC

Speech is turned into text on your own computer. Nothing you say is uploaded.

Only listens for its name

It ignores everything until a phrase starts with "Sayso". You can pause it any time.

You choose where it types

Turn Sayso off for any app, such as your banking app or a game.

Installing

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because Sayso is new and not yet signed. Click More info → Run anyway. The first launch downloads the speech model (about 150 MB) once.
